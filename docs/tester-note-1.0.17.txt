SPASynth 1.0.17 - what's new since 1.0.16

Most of this round came straight from your feedback. Thank you, keep it coming.

Seeing what's wired up
- Any knob wired into the mod matrix now shows it at a glance. It changes colour the moment a route targets it, whether or not a note is playing and whether or not the depth is still at zero, and a travel arc shows how far that knob can actually be pushed or pulled from where it sits. Drag a matrix depth slider and the arc follows you.
- That indicator colour is picked automatically to contrast with your own accent colours, so it stands out whatever you have chosen, and it changes immediately if you change your accents. If you set up custom colours, expect assigned knobs to look different than they did in the last build. That is deliberate.
- Click the centre of a wired knob and every matrix row feeding it lights up in that same colour, scrolling the first into view. It is a plain click, not a drag, so it never gets in the way of turning the knob. The marking stays while you work and clears when you click elsewhere or press Esc.
- A new route now arrives with its depth already at half, so connecting a source to a destination does something straight away. Routes you have already set, including ones you deliberately zeroed, are never touched, and loading a preset never alters the depths it was saved with.

ASSIGN mode
- You can switch tabs while ASSIGN is on. Previously the overlay swallowed tab clicks, so you could only assign to whatever tab happened to be showing, which made a synth-wide assignment impossible. Filters, envelopes, LFOs and FX are all reachable mid-assignment now.
- The glow comes in stages instead of lighting up everything at once. Turning ASSIGN on lights only the parameters you can pick. Once you pick one, it shows as selected and only the matching side of the matrix lights up next.
- Assigning only half a row now clearly shows that row waiting for its other half, outlined with a ring marking the field still to fill. This was always working as intended, it just never said so, which is why it looked stuck.
- ASSIGN switches itself off once a route is complete, so a quick connection takes one click. Double click it to lock it on, the way caps lock works, and a small padlock shows when locked.
- A row aimed at Organic Chaos RATE dims while chaos SYNC is on, because SYNC replaces the free rate with a tempo division and modulating it does nothing until SYNC goes off. The row is untouched and works again the moment you switch SYNC off.

Oscillators
- Right-click an oscillator's title for Copy to Oscillator B/C and Swap with Oscillator B/C. The loaded sound or wavetable travels with the settings. It does not touch anything you have wired in the matrix.
- Drag audio straight onto an oscillator from the Finder, from Explorer, or from your DAW's browser. Dropping onto one that is not already playing a file switches it to Sample for you. Convolve takes a dropped impulse the same way.

Chaos and effects
- Organic Chaos can lock to your tempo. Switch on SYNC and each drifting parameter keeps its own pace, but those paces become musical divisions of the beat, so pitch might settle once a bar while amplitude resettles once a beat, all of it landing on the grid rather than becoming one stepped wobble. The section renames itself Organized Chaos while on.
- Convolve has a START knob. Turning it up skips further into the loaded sound before the wet signal begins, so you can leave the direct hit and early reflections behind and keep only the long diffuse tail. It also lets you reach a later section of a long recording used as an impulse. It never goes silent even pushed all the way over, and RANDOMIZE ALL keeps it in the first half of its range.

Reading the interface
- Every module with an on/off switch shows its power state in its title: muted grey when off, your accent colour when on. Three oscillators, both filters, Organic Chaos, the arpeggiator and each FX tab. The matrix, envelopes and LFOs are always live so their titles do not change.
- The LOCKS label has an arrow pointing at the lock buttons, and sits better against the window edge.
- The panic button is gone from the header. Your host already has its own way to stop stuck notes. Incoming MIDI panic messages are unaffected.

Worth trying specifically
- Wire up a few routes and look at the panel with nothing playing. Every wired knob should stand out.
- Click a wired knob and confirm it finds its row, including a knob fed by two rows at once.
- Turn ASSIGN on and switch tabs mid-assignment, which was impossible before.
- Assign only a destination to an empty row and confirm the waiting state reads clearly rather than looking stuck.
- Open a preset saved in an earlier build and confirm it sounds the same.

Install notes
- macOS: signed and notarized. If the new version does not show up, Plug-in Manager > Reset & Rescan Selection, then restart Logic.
- Windows: unsigned. Click More info, then Run anyway at the SmartScreen prompt.
